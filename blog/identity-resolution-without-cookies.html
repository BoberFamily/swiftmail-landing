Key Patterns for Server-Side Identity Resolution

There are several server-side patterns that businesses can use for identity resolution. Five common patterns include data warehousing, CDPs, identity graphs, device fingerprinting, and probabilistic matching. Data warehousing involves storing customer data in a centralized repository. This enables businesses to create comprehensive customer profiles. CDPs, on the other hand, provide a unified view of customer data. This facilitates more accurate identity resolution. According to industry-research, these patterns can help businesses achieve more accurate and persistent user identification. It's simple.

Another key pattern is identity graphs. These involve creating a visual representation of customer relationships and interactions. This enables businesses to identify patterns and connections that may not be immediately apparent. Device fingerprinting, which involves collecting data on devices used to access a website or application, can also help with identity resolution. Finally, probabilistic matching involves using statistical models to match customer data across different sources. By leveraging these server-side patterns, businesses can create a more comprehensive view of their customers. This enables more effective identity resolution.
