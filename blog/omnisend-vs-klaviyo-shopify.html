Omnisend vs Klaviyo for Shopify Stores with Tiered Billing

January 1, 1970 · 8 min read

Understanding the Landscape of Email Marketing for Shopify

Email marketing is crucial. It drives sales. Shopify acknowledges this. As a Shopify store owner, you're likely familiar with the two leading email marketing platforms: Omnisend and Klaviyo. Both integrate seamlessly with Shopify, but which one is right for your business? Our SwiftMail data shows that a significant portion of abandonment is price-related, highlighting the need for effective email marketing strategies. I've worked with numerous clients who've struggled to choose between Omnisend and Klaviyo, and in this article, we'll delve into the key differences and similarities between these two platforms. It's a big decision. Many factors are at play.

Key Features and Pricing Comparison

Omnisend and Klaviyo both offer a range of features, including segmentation, personalization, and analytics. However, their pricing models differ significantly. Klaviyo is generally more expensive than Omnisend, especially for small to medium-sized Shopify stores, according to industry-research. Omnisend, on the other hand, offers a more affordable pricing plan, with a free plan available for stores with limited features. Our analysis suggests that Klaviyo's advanced automation features come at a cost, with prices increasing exponentially as your subscriber list grows. In contrast, Omnisend's pricing remains relatively stable, making it a more attractive option for smaller stores. It's cheaper. The Cost Crossover Point: Where Omnisend and Klaviyo Diverge

The cost crossover point between Omnisend and Klaviyo is a critical threshold for Shopify stores. According to industry-research, this point is around a certain subscriber count. Below this mark, Omnisend is generally more affordable, while Klaviyo becomes more cost-effective for larger stores. This is because Klaviyo's pricing model is based on the number of subscribers, with costs increasing as your list grows. In contrast, Omnisend offers a more tiered pricing structure, with costs increasing at a slower rate. Our SwiftMail data shows that a notable portion of customer journeys involve multiple sessions, highlighting the importance of choosing an email marketing platform that can handle large volumes of data. Free Plans and Trials: What Omnisend and Klaviyo Offer

Omnisend offers a free plan with limited features, while Klaviyo provides a free trial but no free plan. This is an important distinction, as it can significantly impact your decision. If you're just starting out or have a small store, Omnisend's free plan may be sufficient for your needs. However, if you're looking for more advanced features, Klaviyo's free trial may be a better option. According to esp-docs, Klaviyo's free trial includes access to all features, allowing you to test the platform before committing to a paid plan. It's a good option. Our experience suggests that Omnisend's free plan is a great way to get started with email marketing, but it's essential to upgrade to a paid plan as your store grows.

Segmentation and Personalization: A Deep Dive

Both Omnisend and Klaviyo offer segmentation and personalization features for email campaigns. However, Klaviyo's automation features are more advanced, allowing for more complex segmentation and targeting. According to esp-docs, Klaviyo's segmentation features include behavioral targeting, demographic targeting, and firmographic targeting. In contrast, Omnisend's segmentation features are more limited, but still effective for smaller stores. Our SwiftMail data shows that a small fraction of form friction is due to poor segmentation, highlighting the importance of choosing an email marketing platform that can handle complex segmentation. Analytics and Reporting: Tracking Performance with Omnisend and Klaviyo

Both Omnisend and Klaviyo provide analytics and reporting features to track email campaign performance. According to Shopify, these features are essential for optimizing your email marketing strategy and driving sales. Our analysis suggests that Klaviyo's analytics features are more advanced, with detailed reports on open rates, click-through rates, and conversion rates. In contrast, Omnisend's analytics features are more limited, but still provide valuable insights into your email campaign performance. It's easy to use. For more information on analytics and reporting, check out our guide on email marketing analytics.

Making the Choice: Omnisend or Klaviyo for Your Shopify Store

Ultimately, the choice between Omnisend and Klaviyo depends on your specific needs and budget. If you're a small to medium-sized Shopify store with limited budget, Omnisend may be the better option. However, if you're a larger store with more complex email marketing needs, Klaviyo may be the better choice.
